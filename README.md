### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Computer Science and Engineering** undergraduate at *Government Engineering College, Kannur*, backed by a Diploma in **Electronics and Communication Engineering** (CGPA: 8.77).

My background bridges hardware intuition and low-level software engineering: from industrial wire harnessing and government IT operations to building high-performance developer tooling in **Rust**, **Go**, and **C/C++**.

- 🦀 **Systems & Tooling:** Passionate about building fast, reliable command-line and terminal user interfaces (TUI) with clean architectures (`Ratatui`, `Tokio`, `Glamour`).
- 🐧 **Linux & OS Internals:** Dedicated Arch Linux + Hyprland power user; focused on process scheduling, memory virtualization, Unix pipes, and system APIs.
- 🔌 **Electronics & Embedded Systems:** Experienced in circuit prototyping, USB power negotiation, reverse-current protection, RFID systems, and ESP32 home automation.
- 💼 **Industry Background:** Past experience in government network supervision at **KELTRON**, industrial electronics at **SFO Technologies**, and AI development at **Infosys Springboard**.
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Rust, Go, OS internals, Arch Linux customization, Neovim, and hardware DIY.

### 🧠 Core Engineering Focus

- **Systems & OS Internals:** Process scheduling (FCFS, SJF, Priority, Round Robin), deadlock avoidance (Banker's Algorithm), disk scheduling (FCFS, SCAN, C-SCAN), virtual memory management & paging.
- **Low-Level Architecture:** x86/8086 Assembly, NASM syntax, SIC/XE architecture & two-pass assembler design, addressing modes, and object code generation.
- **Networking & Protocols:** IEEE 802.11 frames, Ethernet protocols, OSI & TCP/IP stack mapping, CIDR subnetting, VLSM routing, DSC authentication workflows, and socket communication.
- **Hardware & Power Electronics:** Circuit debugging, USB power & pinout analysis, 5V power injection with 1N4007 backflow diode protection, RFID validation systems, and wire harness manufacturing.
